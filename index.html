SIMPLE STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Play Telenor Quiz?

Follow these simple steps to find the quiz in the My Telenor App.

01

Download My Telenor App

Install the official My Telenor App from Google Play Store.

02

Open the Application

Open the app and sign in using your Telenor number.

03

Find Quiz or Rewards

Look for available quiz, games or rewards sections.

04

Open the Available Quiz

If a quiz is available for your account, open it and read its instructions.

05

Read the Questions

Read each question and check all four answer options.

06

Select Your Answer

Choose the option you believe is correct and submit it.

07

Check Your Result

Follow the result or reward instructions shown inside the app.

Important: Quiz availability, rewards and rules are controlled by Telenor. They may change or may not be available to every account.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is this website?

This is an independent website that displays daily quiz questions, options and answers added to its answers file.

2. Are answers updated automatically?

The website loads answers automatically from answers.json. Actual questions and answers must be added and updated by the website owner.

3. Why are today's answers not showing?

The answers file may not have been updated, the date may be incorrect, or the file may contain an error.

4. How can I find free MB offers?

Check the official My Telenor App for offers available to your account. Availability and conditions can change.

5. Is this the official Telenor website?

No. This is an independent quiz information website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telenor Pakistan.
